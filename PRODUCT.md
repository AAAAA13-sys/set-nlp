# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Students** (primary, largest group). Every semester they must evaluate each teacher of each enrolled course during an evaluation window set by the administrator. They use both phones and school/lab computers; neither device can be treated as secondary.
- **Deans and department chairs.** Monitor who has finished and who has not (completion status only, scoped to their college or department) and read anonymous results and written-feedback analysis to plan faculty development.
- **Faculty.** Read their own anonymous results, but only after their term grades are submitted and locked by the registrar.
- **Administrator / registrar.** Sets the evaluation window (dates, Asia/Manila), imports rosters, manages terms, courses, sections and users, and locks grades.

## Product Purpose

EvaluaTech runs the mandatory Student Evaluation of Teachers (SET) for School AAAAA13. Success means every enrolled student finishes every evaluation within the window, supervisors can see and follow up on who has not, and faculty receive honest, anonymous feedback with a trustworthy sentiment and topic analysis of written comments.

## Positioning

Students' identities and their answers are stored separately (`evaluation_clearances` vs `evaluation_vault`), so the school can track who finished without ever being able to link a student to what they wrote. Written feedback in English, Tagalog and Taglish is analysed by an in-house NLP/sentiment model.

## Operating Context

- One evaluation per enrolled course section: 25 Likert items in five clusters (Pedagogical Delivery, Assessment & Grading, Classroom Management, Professional Conduct, Student Support & Mentorship) plus written feedback.
- The administrator-controlled window (`starts_at`, `ends_at`) is the only timing gate; students may submit as soon as they are done.
- Faculty results stay locked until grades are submitted and registrar-locked (double-blind).
- Zero Permits Policy: evaluations never gate exams or permits. The product tracks participation (done vs. pending) for follow-up only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack already chosen: Laravel 12 / standalone PDO backend, Vue 3 + Pinia + Vue Router SPA built with Vite, Python/Flask NLP microservice.
- Five roles: student, faculty, chair, dean, admin. Chairs are scoped to a department, deans to a college.
- The NLP service must never block submissions; the backend falls back to a local lexicon classifier.
- No demo shortcuts, role switchers or auto-login buttons in production.

## Evidence on Hand

- Training data: `docs/Student Evaluation of Teachers Dataset ... - Dataset.csv` (10,000 rows, of which 800 are real student comments from `... Dataset.bak.csv`; the remaining 9,200 are template-generated by `scripts/generate_10k_dataset.py`).
- Model accuracy must be reported on **real** student comments (user decision, 2026-11-11). Synthetic-set scores may appear only when clearly labelled as synthetic.
- No real testimonials, customer logos or adoption figures exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Finishing an evaluation should be fast and calm on any device; never punish or shame a student.
2. Anonymity is visible: every screen that shows completion status also says that answers are not linked to students.
3. Supervisors see status and aggregates, never individual responses.
4. Numbers shown to people are measured, not marketed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2, targeting AAA text contrast (7:1) per project rules; full keyboard operation; 44px tap targets on phones; respects reduced motion. Students write in English, Tagalog and Taglish.
